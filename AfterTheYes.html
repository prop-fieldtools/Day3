The signature starts the handoff.

After the yes, the Pilot stays connected to the merchant while Partner Development, Underwriting and Operations guide the account toward approval and launch.

Submit a complete file

Review signatures, disclosures, equipment selections and required documentation before submission. Complete files reduce avoidable delays.

Underwriting reviews the opportunity

The review considers the business model, ownership, transaction profile, financial exposure and other factors required by current policy. The Pilot does not make the risk decision.

Respond to pends clearly

A pend is a request for more information, not a no. Coordinate with Dan, explain the request clearly and keep the communication loop open without interpreting underwriting policy.

Confirm the next operational step

Approval allows boarding and deployment work to begin. Verify equipment, configuration, shipping and installation expectations.

The Pilot protects the relationship.
